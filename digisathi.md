# DigiSathi – Privacy Policy



DigiSathi  is a personal document-organizing application designed to help users securely organize and access important documents such as identity documents, financial documents, property documents, certificates, and other personal records.

Your privacy is important to us. DigiSathi is designed with a **local-first approach**, meaning your documents are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

## 1. Information We Store

DigiSathi may allow you to add and organize information such as:

* Aadhaar and other identity documents
* PAN documents
* Bank-related documents
* Property documents
* Certificates
* Address information
* Personal notes and document details
* Photos or PDF files selected by you

This information is stored **locally on your device**, subject to the storage and security mechanisms provided by your device and operating system.

## 2. We Do Not Upload Your Documents

DigiSathi does not upload your personal documents, photos, PDFs, or document information to our servers.

Your documents remain on your device unless you intentionally use an operating-system or third-party feature to share, back up, export, or transfer them.

## 3. Personal Information

DigiSathi does not require you to create an account in order to use the core document-storage functionality.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information contained in your documents, including Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, bank account information, property information, or other document contents.

## 4. Camera and Photo/Storage Access

Depending on the features you use, DigiSathi may request access to:

**Camera:**
If you choose to scan or photograph a document using the camera, camera access is used only for that purpose.

**Photos/Files/Storage:**
If you choose to import a document or image, DigiSathi may request access to the relevant files or photos on your device.

These permissions are used only to provide the functionality you request.

## 5. Location Information

If DigiSathi provides a feature for saving or organizing a location associated with a document, location access may be requested.

Location access is used only when you choose to use a location-related feature. DigiSathi does not continuously track your location.

## 6. Data Sharing

DigiSathi does not sell, rent, or share your personal documents or document contents with third parties.

When you explicitly choose to share a document using Android's sharing functionality, the document is transferred to the application or service you select. Such third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

## 7. Data Security

DigiSathi is designed to keep your documents on your device and minimize unnecessary transmission of sensitive information.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with a strong screen lock, PIN, password, or biometric authentication and keeping your device software updated.

If DigiSathi provides an optional app lock or other security feature, users should enable it for additional protection.

## 8. Data Backup and Device Transfer

Because your documents are stored locally, uninstalling DigiSathi, clearing its application data, resetting your device, or losing your device may result in loss of locally stored information.

Users are responsible for maintaining any backup they choose to create.

If you intentionally export or back up documents using another service, that service's privacy policy and security practices will apply.

## 9. Children's Privacy

DigiSathi is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through DigiSathi.

## 10. Third-Party Services

DigiSathi may use third-party services necessary for application functionality, distribution, diagnostics, crash reporting, analytics, or other technical purposes.

Any third-party service used by DigiSathi will process information according to its own privacy policy and applicable laws.

Where possible, DigiSathi is designed to minimize the amount of personal or sensitive information shared with such services.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to DigiSathi, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the beginning of this policy.
