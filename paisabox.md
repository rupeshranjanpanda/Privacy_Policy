# PaisaBox Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 20 September 2026  
**App:** PaisaBox  
**Developer:** Rupesh

PaisaBox is a personal finance application designed to help users track income and expenses automatically from relevant transaction SMS messages. PaisaBox is built with a privacy-first approach: financial information processed by the app is intended to remain on the user's device and is not uploaded to a developer-controlled cloud server.

## 1. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions you grant, PaisaBox may access:

### SMS / Transaction Messages
PaisaBox may read SMS messages on your device to identify relevant financial transactions such as credits, debits, payments, transfers, and other transaction information.

The app uses this information only to provide its core personal-finance functionality, such as:
- Automatically identifying income and expenses
- Creating transaction records
- Categorising transactions
- Showing spending and income summaries
- Providing financial insights and reports

PaisaBox does not need access to unrelated SMS content for its core functionality.

### Financial Information
Transaction information identified from SMS messages may include amounts, transaction dates, transaction descriptions, merchant names, account/bank identifiers appearing in a transaction message, and transaction type.

### Information You Enter
If you manually add, edit, categorise, or delete transactions, that information is stored locally on your device.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The information accessed by PaisaBox is used solely to provide and improve the app's personal finance features.

Examples include:
- Tracking daily income and expenses
- Maintaining transaction history
- Calculating savings and spending
- Creating monthly and other financial summaries
- Generating charts and insights
- Helping you understand your spending patterns

PaisaBox does **not** use your financial or SMS information for advertising, profiling, selling data, or unrelated purposes.

## 3. Local Storage and Privacy

PaisaBox is designed around local-first data storage.

Your transaction records and financial information are stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally upload your SMS messages, transaction history, or financial records to a PaisaBox developer-controlled server.

Because the information is stored locally, your data may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its app data, lose your device, or otherwise remove the locally stored information without creating your own backup.

## 4. Data Sharing

PaisaBox does **not sell, rent, or share your personal financial information or SMS transaction data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The app does not intentionally transmit your SMS history or financial transaction records to the developer.

If a future version introduces a third-party service or cloud feature that requires transmission of personal or financial data, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the relevant disclosure and consent requirements will be followed before such processing.

## 5. SMS Permission and User Consent

PaisaBox requests SMS access only because automatic transaction tracking is a core feature of the application.

The permission is optional from the user's perspective. You can decline the permission, but automatic transaction detection from SMS will not work without the required Android permission.

PaisaBox processes SMS information for the financial-management functionality described in this policy and does not use the permission for unrelated purposes.

## 6. Data Security

PaisaBox uses a local-storage approach to reduce unnecessary transmission of sensitive financial information.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their device, including device lock, PIN, password, biometrics, and other security controls.

No application can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their device and operating system updated and use appropriate device security.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because PaisaBox stores financial information locally, the user controls the locally stored data.

You can delete locally stored transaction data using the deletion or reset options provided by the app, where available. You can also remove the app and its local application data through Android settings.

PaisaBox does not maintain a developer-controlled database of your personal financial records.

PaisaBox does not currently require users to create an online account. Therefore, there is no developer-hosted account database to delete.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PaisaBox is intended for general personal-finance use and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-controlled online service. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact the developer using the contact information below.

## 9. Third-Party Services

PaisaBox is designed to operate without requiring a developer-controlled cloud account for its core transaction-tracking functionality.

If future versions integrate third-party services, analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, or other services that process user data, the relevant data practices will be disclosed and this policy will be updated as necessary.

## 10. Your Choices

You can:
- Deny SMS permission
- Revoke SMS permission later through Android settings
- Delete locally stored transactions where the app provides the relevant option
- Clear the app's local data through Android settings
- Uninstall PaisaBox

Revoking SMS permission will prevent automatic SMS-based transaction detection.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PaisaBox's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through the app and the public Privacy Policy page used for the Google Play listing. The effective date at the top of this policy will indicate when the policy was last updated.

## Important Google Play Publishing Note

PaisaBox uses SMS-based transaction detection as a core feature. Google Play treats SMS permissions as high-risk/restricted permissions and requires eligible apps to comply with the SMS/Call Log Permissions policy and, where applicable, complete the Permissions Declaration process. Google Play currently lists **SMS-based money management (for example, apps that track and manage budgets)** as an exception use case subject to review and approval.

The app's Play Console declarations, in-app disclosure, permission request, Privacy Policy, and actual implementation should all accurately describe the same SMS-based financial-management functionality.
